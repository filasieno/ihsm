A single recorded interaction: an event name plus its payload.

Produced both by [testing!TestPort](../../testing/classes/TestPort.mdx) (the messages a test double records) and by the
[testing!TestActor](../../testing/type-aliases/TestActor.mdx) `subscribe` observer stream (every event posted through the machine).

## Properties

### event

> `readonly` **event**: `string`

Event/service name, or a free-form label recorded by a [testing!TestPort](../../testing/classes/TestPort.mdx).

***

### payload

> `readonly` **payload**: readonly `unknown`[]

Arguments captured with the event (a defensive copy).
