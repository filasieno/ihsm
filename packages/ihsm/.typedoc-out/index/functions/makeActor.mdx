> **makeActor**\<`Context`, `Public`, `Internal`, `P`\>(`topState`, `ctx`, `port`, `options?`, ...`_disjointGuard`): [`Actor`](../type-aliases/Actor.mdx)\<`Context`, `Public`\>

Creates an actor exposing only its **public** protocol, with an optional outbound [Port](../classes/Port.mdx).

Like [makeHsm](makeHsm.mdx) but separates the public, client-callable protocol from an
`InternalProtocol` that only the port may post inward. The returned [Actor](../type-aliases/Actor.mdx) surfaces
just the public events; handlers (and the port) may post the merged [Dispatch](../type-aliases/Dispatch.mdx) protocol.

The trailing `Disjoint` guard is a compile-time gate: if `Public` and `Internal` share an event
name, the call fails to type-check, pointing at the overlapping keys.
