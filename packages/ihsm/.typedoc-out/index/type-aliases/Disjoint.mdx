> **Disjoint**\<`Public`, `Internal`\> = `Extract`\<keyof `Public`, keyof `Internal`\> *extends* `never` ? `true` : \[`"ihsm: public and internal protocols must not share keys"`, `Extract`\<keyof `Public`, keyof `Internal`\>\]

Compile-time guard asserting the public and internal protocols share **no** event names.

Resolves to `true` when `keyof Public` and `keyof Internal` are disjoint; otherwise to a
descriptive tuple that fails the `extends true` constraint on [makeActor](../functions/makeActor.mdx) /
[testing!makeTestActor](../../testing/functions/makeTestActor.mdx), surfacing the overlapping keys at the call site.
